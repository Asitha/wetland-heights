Brand Story

The Wetland Heights visual identity draws from the natural landscape of Sri Lanka — the warm tones of Ceylon cinnamon, the cool teals of urban wetland water, and the earthy neutrals of tropical soil and timber. Our palette connects guests to place before they arrive.

Your home in the heart of Nugegoda

Wetland Heights is a modern, fully-furnished apartment overlooking the Diyawanna Oya wetlands in Nugegoda, Sri Lanka. Designed with natural materials and warm tones, every detail connects you to the lush landscape just beyond the window. Whether you're visiting for a weekend or settling in for a longer stay, this is a space that feels like home.

Social Media

  • Use Cream (#F8F5F1) backgrounds for posts and stories
  • Set typography in Cinnamon tones for warmth
  • Apply Wetland Teal as accent color for icons, borders, and highlights
  • Maintain 60/30/10 ratio even in small-format assets

Photography Overlays

  • Use semi-transparent Cream overlays (#F8F5F1 at 85% opacity) for text legibility
  • Avoid dark gradient overlays — they conflict with the warm, airy brand feel
  • Photos should feel light, natural, and grounded in the Sri Lankan landscape
