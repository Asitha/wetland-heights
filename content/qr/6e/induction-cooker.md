## 1. Power On

Touch the **ON/OFF** button (center bottom). The display shows "- -" — the cooktop is in standby.

## 2. Place a Pot & Select the Zone

Put one of our provided pots on any cooking zone. Tap the **zone selector** next to it — the display blinks, meaning it's ready for input.

## 3. Set the Heat

Tap the **slider** near your desired level (near 0 = low, near 9 = high). The blinking number confirms your setting. You can change it anytime while cooking.

## 4. When Done

Tap the **zone selector** for the zone you used, then tap the slider near **0**. Turn off the cooktop with the **ON/OFF** button. The cooling fan may run for a minute after — that's completely normal.

> [!tip] Display Showing Something Weird?
> - **"U" flashing** — The pot isn't compatible with induction. Switch to one of the pots we've provided.
> - **Controls not responding** — Wipe the touch panel dry. Even a thin film of water disables the controls. If still unresponsive, the child lock may be on — press and hold the lock icon for a few seconds.
> - **"H" showing** — The cooking zone surface is still hot. Wait before touching or cleaning.
> - **Fan still running after turning off** — This is the cooling fan. Completely normal. It stops on its own.

:::details Heat Settings Guide
**These are guidelines** — experiment to find what works best for you.

| Setting | What to Cook |
|---------|-------------|
| 1–2 | Melt butter or chocolate, warm sauces, gentle simmering |
| 3–4 | Reheat food, simmer soups, cook rice |
| 5–6 | Pancakes, eggs |
| 7–8 | Sauteing, cooking pasta |
| 9 | Stir-fry, sear meat, boil water fast |

**Tip:** Start on high to heat up, then reduce. Using a lid saves energy and cooks faster.
:::

:::details Handy Features
- **Boost:** Tap a zone selector, then tap the **Boost** button — the zone runs at maximum power for 5 minutes, then returns to the previous setting. Great for boiling water fast.
- **Timer:** Tap the **Timer** button — the same slider now controls minutes instead of heat. Set your time (up to 99 min). The zone switches off automatically when the timer expires.
- **Child Lock:** Press and hold the **Lock** button for a few seconds. All controls are disabled except ON/OFF. Hold again to unlock.
:::

:::details Cleaning Tips
- **Wipe while warm** (not hot) — dried-on spills are harder to remove later.
- Use a **damp cloth** or mild cooktop cleaner. No abrasive scourers or steel wool.
- If something spills on the **touch controls**, the panel may stop responding. Wipe dry before trying again.
- **Sugar or melted plastic** on the glass? Scrape carefully with a flat blade while still warm — once cooled, they can bond permanently.
:::

:::details Is My Pot Induction-Compatible?
The pots and pan we've provided all work. But if you bring your own:

- **Magnet test:** If a magnet sticks to the bottom, it works on induction.
- **Look for the symbol:** A coil icon on the base or packaging means induction-compatible.
- **What doesn't work:** Pure aluminium, copper, glass, ceramic, and non-magnetic stainless steel.
- **Size matters:** Pots smaller than 14 cm may not be detected by the cooktop.
:::

> [!warn] Please Do Not
> - Use **abrasive cleaners or scourers** on the glass surface
> - Place anything other than cookware on the surface while cooking (phones, utensils, foil)
> - **Slide pots** across the glass — lift them instead to avoid scratches
> - Leave the cooktop **unattended on high heat** — it will auto-shut off eventually, but don't rely on this
> - Place **credit cards or electronics** near the cooktop — the magnetic field can damage them
