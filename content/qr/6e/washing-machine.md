## 1. Load Your Clothes

Open the door and place your clothes inside.
Don't overfill — leave a hand's width of space at the top so clothes can tumble freely.
Close the door firmly until you hear it click.

## 2. Add Detergent

Pull out the drawer from the top-left of the machine:

:::drawer-table
| LEFT (Large) :: Main Wash | BACK RIGHT (Blue) :: Fabric Softener | FRONT RIGHT :: Pre-Wash / Bleach |
|---|---|---|
| Pour liquid or powder detergent here. | Small amount of softener (optional) | You probably won't need this |
| *If using liquid, it may drain into the machine immediately. This is normal.* | *Do not fill past the MAX line. It will drain too early.* | |
:::

> [!pod] Using Pods?
> Place the pod directly in the drum with your clothes. Do NOT put pods in the drawer.

Detergent is provided on the shelf. Extra available at Food City (5 min walk on Galle Road).

## 3. Select a Wash Cycle

Press the Power button. Then turn the dial to select your cycle:

:::cycles
- **Quick 30** — ~30 min
- **Mixed Fabric** — everyday
- **Cotton** — towels & sheets
:::

## 4. Start the Wash

Press the Start/Pause button (the &#x25B6;&#x2759; symbol).
The door locks automatically. Do NOT force the door open while the machine is running.

## 5. When Finished

The machine plays a melody when the cycle ends.
Wait about 1 minute for the door lock to release, then remove your clothes.

:::section ☀️ Drying
A drying rack is available. You can place it on the balcony — be mindful of the wind! Clothes dry quickly in the Sri Lankan climate.
:::

> [!tip] ✨ Help Us Keep It Fresh
> - **Air It Out:** After your final load, leave the washer door and the cabinet door slightly ajar to prevent moisture buildup.
> - ⚠️ But please close both doors before nightfall — the washer is on the balcony and insects can get in overnight.
> - **Wipe the Seal:** If you notice any hair or lint trapped in the grey rubber ring around the door, a quick wipe with a paper towel is greatly appreciated.
> - **Detergent Drawer:** If any powder or liquid spills on the outside of the drawer, a quick rinse or wipe prevents it from hardening.

> [!warn] Please Do Not
> - Force the door open while the machine is running
> - Overload the drum — max 8 kg (about half-full looks right)
> - Use excessive detergent — more soap ≠ cleaner clothes
> - Wash shoes or items with metal parts that could damage the drum
> - Leave wet clothes in the machine for hours — they'll smell
